How we check

Short version: we only read the vendor's own pages, we quote them word for word, and we date everything.

  1. Official pages only. Terms of service, paid-plan terms, privacy and biometric policies, acceptable-use and moderation policies, security pages and pricing pages.
  2. One quote per answer. Each answer keeps the exact sentence, the URL and the date it was read. If a page did not load or did not say it, the answer is Not found. We never fill gaps from third-party sites.
  3. Three kinds of answer. Verified the rule is written; inferred our reading of a pricing table (for example “No watermarks” listed only on paid plans); Not found.
  4. Conflicts shown, not hidden. When terms of service and paid-plan terms disagree (Kling, Hailuo) we quote the clause we relied on and say which document is newer.
  5. Weekly re-checks (planned). A changed sentence updates the date and goes into a public change log.
  6. Vendor claims are labelled. Certifications (SOC 2, ISO) are as stated by each vendor; we have not seen the audit reports.

  • MiniMax platform terms: empty page.
